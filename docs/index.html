Choose transfer mode

MODE A · WHOLE LIBRARY

Partner Sharing checklist

Open Google Photos settings

In Account A choose Partner Sharing → All time → All photos and invite Account B. Then Account B accepts and chooses Save to your account → All photos.

PLANNED

Partner Sharing moves media, not arbitrary album organization. If preserving album structure matters, record important albums as separate Shared Album batches below.

MODE B · SELECTED MEDIA

Shared Album batches

Open Google Photos

In Account A select the media, create a shared album using the generated batch name, and share it directly with Account B. In Account B open the album and choose Save all photos and videos.
